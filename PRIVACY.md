# Privacy Policy — MaxVideo Pro

**Effective Date:** October 31, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 31, 2026  

MaxVideo Pro ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our browser extension operates and how we handle user data.

---

### 1. Data Collection and Usage

**MaxVideo Pro does NOT collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or user identifiers.**

All operations, media controls, layout enhancements, and downloads performed by MaxVideo Pro run **100% locally inside your browser client**. We operate no analytics servers, no user tracking services, no external advertising networks, and no external telemetry databases.

---

### 2. Permissions and Purpose

MaxVideo Pro requests the following permissions strictly to provide its core video enhancement functionality:

* **`tabs`**: Required to identify the active tab displaying a video and transfer it into a dedicated, distraction-free "Clean Window" pop-out window without address bars, tabs, or bookmarks, and restore it back to the main browser window when exited.
* **`storage`**: Used solely to store your personal user preferences locally on your device (such as your chosen playback speed, default zoom/fit mode, audio boost percentage, and HUD toolbar visibility). This data never leaves your device.
* **`contextMenus`**: Allows you to right-click on web videos to trigger Clean Window mode, Fullscreen, or Picture-in-Picture directly from the browser's context menu.
* **`scripting`**: Required to inject lightweight framing styles and adjust video sizing in the web player when you activate Clean Window mode.
* **`downloads`**: Used to save the media stream file (video/audio) directly into your device's local Downloads folder when you explicitly request a download.
* **`declarativeNetRequest`**: Used to configure dynamic header modifications locally so that browser security rules do not block media segment requests initiated directly by the user.
* **Host Permissions (`<all_urls>`)**: Required so that you can maximize and enhance HTML5 video players on any website across the web (such as news portals, online course platforms, social media, and video streaming sites).

---

### 3. Third-Party Services & Remote Code

* MaxVideo Pro does **NOT** load any remote JavaScript or WebAssembly code.
* All scripts, stylesheets, and icons are completely bundled inside the extension package.
* We do not share, sell, or rent any information to third-party data brokers, advertising networks, or marketing companies.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy

MaxVideo Pro does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children or any other individual, as no data collection mechanism exists within the software.
